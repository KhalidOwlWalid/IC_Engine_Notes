## P-v diagram

- Work is the output of any heat engine.
- Work is given by:
$$
W = \int F dx = \int PA_p  \ dx
$$
where P is pressure in combustion chamber, $A_p$ is the area against the piston face and x is the distance the piston moves.

Because engines are often multicylinder, it is convenient to analyze engine per unit mass of gas, m within the cylinder.

In this case, the volume V is replaved with specific volume and work is replaved with specific work:

$$
w = W/m \qquad v= V/m
$$
Hence, specific work is equal to the area under the process lines on the P-v coordinates given in the above image.

### Reading the P-v diagram
![[Pasted image 20260719223659.png]]
The given diagram is for the throttle being wide-open. In chronological order:
1. Piston is at Bottom dead center whereby the pressure shown here is the pressure inside the combustion chamber. At BDC, while the exhaust valve is opened, as the piston moves from BDC to TDC, it pushes the air out. At this point of time, the exhaust is at around atmospheric pressure.
2. Once the piston is at TDC, the exhaust valve closes, and the intake valve opens, which sucks the air into the combustion chamber, the pressure slowly drops below atmospheric pressure since the volume of the combustion chamber increases as the piston moves back to BDC.
3. Once it reaches BDC, and the intake valve closes, all of the air inside the combustion chamber is compressed as the piston moves to TDC.
4. Once it reaches TDC, fuel is injected and ignition occured which led the the fuel combustion and quickly builds up pressure inside the chamber.
5. This then pushes the piston to BDC, which resulted in the pressure dropping over time since the volume in the cylinder grows. Cycle then repeats.

![[Pasted image 20260719224640.png]]
- In this plot, this is the P-v diagram for when the throttle is partly opened.
- The pressure during the intake cycle is relatively lower than when the throttle is wide open, since there is less air going into the combustion chamber due to the throttle restriction.
- While the piston moves down to BDC, the volume of the combustion chamber grows which inherently causes the pressure to drop lower than when the throttle is wide open.
